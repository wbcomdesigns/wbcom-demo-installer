Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about the demo installer

Is the demo installer sold separately?

No, the demo installer is exclusively included with the Reign theme purchase. It cannot be purchased or used separately.

Will it work with other themes?

The demo installer is specifically designed for the Reign theme and will only work when Reign is activated as your WordPress theme.

Can I use it on multiple sites?

Yes, you can use the demo installer on all sites covered by your Reign theme license. Check your license terms for the number of allowed sites.

Does it work with BuddyBoss?

Yes! The demo installer fully supports both BuddyPress and BuddyBoss Platform, automatically detecting and configuring for either platform.

How long does import take?

Import times vary based on demo size and server performance. Small demos take 2-5 minutes, while larger demos may take 10-20 minutes.

Is support included?

Yes, support for the demo installer is included with your Reign theme support license. Our team is ready to help with any issues.
